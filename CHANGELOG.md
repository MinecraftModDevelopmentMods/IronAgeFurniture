# Iron Age Furniture 0.4.1.121011

- Bring the Phase 4 furniture and lighting improvements to Forge 1.21.1.
- Add wingback chairs, thrones, wooden beds, double beds and canopies in all sixteen colours.
- Keep removable shields and their saved details, bare chair frames, and sconces with up to four mixed-colour candles.
- Include the refined recipe-book entries, ingredient discovery, four creative tabs and server-controlled settings.
- Retain all eleven vanilla, thirteen Biomes O' Plenty and twenty-five Oh The Biomes We've Gone wood families.
- Use five copper ingots for 32 sconces when copper nuggets are unavailable; five nuggets still make four sconces.
- Keep waterlogging, redstone, buckets, support-loss drops, lava lamps and fire-resistant metal frames.
- Keep the glass on placed lamps and sconces transparent in Fast and Fancy graphics.
- Carry forward older furniture and saved-item recovery using the Minecraft 1.21.1 save format.

Back up existing worlds and try upgrades on a copy first. Worlds from newer Minecraft versions cannot be downgraded.

# IronAgeFurniture 0.3.0.121011

- Port IronAgeFurniture to Minecraft 1.21.1 and Forge 52.1.0 on Java 21.
- Retain the complete contemporary Biomes O' Plenty furniture family and its conditional recipes and advancements.
- Restore Oh The Biomes We've Gone furniture, including Spirit wood, while continuing to omit Immersive Engineering on this target.
- Correct every BOP and BWG model to use its matching target-native texture path and validate the mappings against the pinned integration jars.
- Update iron-sconce models to Minecraft's current `block/iron_block` texture path.
- Carry forward ingredient-specific recipe advancements, normal wooden-furniture flammability with fireproof Nether woods, and the restored throwable/shattering lava-lamp behavior.
- Carry forward the deterministic checked-in furniture catalog, scoped BOP/BYG remaps, and dedicated-server checks for optional furniture.

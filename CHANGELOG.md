# Iron Age Furniture 0.4.1.121111

- Bring the Phase 4 furniture and lighting improvements to Forge 1.21.11.
- Add wingback chairs, thrones, wooden beds, double beds and canopies in all sixteen colours.
- Keep removable shields and their saved details, bare empty chair frames, and sconces with up to four mixed-colour candles.
- Include the refined recipes, ingredient discovery, four creative tabs and server-controlled gameplay settings.
- Retain all twelve vanilla, fourteen Biomes O' Plenty and twenty-five Oh The Biomes We've Gone wood families.
- Correct the log ingredients in BWG log-bench recipes.
- Keep waterlogging, redstone, buckets, support-loss drops, lava lamps and fire-resistant metal frames.
- Keep the glass on placed lamps and sconces transparent in Fast and Fancy graphics.
- Carry forward older furniture and saved-item recovery using the Minecraft 1.21.11 save format.

Back up existing worlds and try upgrades on a copy first. Worlds from newer Minecraft versions cannot be downgraded.

# IronAgeFurniture 0.3.0.121111

- Port IronAgeFurniture to Minecraft 1.21.11 and Forge 61.1.0 on Java 21.
- Add Pale Oak to the vanilla furniture families and Origin Oak to the contemporary Biomes O' Plenty furniture family.
- Retain Oh The Biomes We've Gone furniture, including Spirit wood, while continuing to omit Immersive Engineering on this target.
- Validate built-in texture paths and every optional wood model semantically against the exact pinned BOP and BWG jars.
- Generate Forge 61 item definitions for every visible item and use target-native recipe ingredients and conditional data.
- Keep the furniture catalog, BOP/BYG name remaps and dedicated-server checks for optional furniture.
- Fix recipe advancements so furniture unlocks only when its actual ingredient is obtained, including conditional optional-mod recipes.
- Restore normal wooden-furniture fire behaviour while keeping Crimson and Warped furniture non-flammable.
- Restore throwable lava lamps, water shattering with the glass sound and obsidean-chunk drop, and safe creative-mode removal.
- Preserve transparent glass and visible contents while glow, lava, and red lamps are falling.
